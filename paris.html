Remembering Gene Svoboda and Magnum Aircraft Engines

Visiting Magnum Aircraft Engines in 2012.

Gene Svoboda (1944–2014) — master engine builder and owner of Magnum Aircraft Engines, which operated first in Dayton, Ohio, then Fort Wayne, Indiana and finally Paris, Texas.

The Overhaul (2000)

Gene overhauled my TIO-540-S1AD engine in 2000. This was the second time this engine was field overhauled using the same case and crankshaft. I was able to run it beyond the 1800 hour recommended TBO until 2023 without ever pulling a cylinder. Only accessory service was performed in that time. Truly a craftsman and wonderful person. Blue skies and eternal tailwinds Gene and Solo.
